## Monograph credit and earlier exports

Copyright 2026 Igor Rivin, Department of Mathematics, Temple University. The monograph acknowledges substantial help from Codex in its preparation and revision and in the computational companions. A suggested attribution is: “Igor Rivin, *Polyhedra, Moduli, and Quantum Geometry*, CC BY 4.0,” with a link to the material and license and an indication of changes as applicable. The license text governs; this suggested form adds no license conditions.

The CC BY 4.0 grant also covers Igor Rivin's monograph text and original figures embodied in preserved earlier monograph source/PDF snapshots and Section 9 chapter exports. Those files retain their original bytes, dates, source identities and evidence status. A mixed archive is not licensed as a whole by this notice. In particular, the grant does not newly license the separate historical `ipoly` study or scripts, third-party code, fonts, reproduced material, or other content for which Igor Rivin has not granted rights. Retain their existing attribution and notices. The [accompanying monograph notice](MONOGRAPH-NOTICE.md) can be distributed beside an unchanged historical download.

## Computational scope and distribution

The exact MIT file list covers the maintained `counting_mixing`, `normalizations`, `quantum_geodesics`, `quantum_surgery`, `section15`, `section15_quantum`, `section30`, `section9` and `torus_campaign` experiment sources, their tests, named current fixtures and associated README files, plus the experiment index. It excludes `experiments/section15/historical-table.json`, `experiments/quantum_geodesics/contracts/`, generated reports/results, archived source snapshots and dependencies. Separately attributed or licensed material retains its own terms even when used by a listed implementation.

Keep the relevant full license text, copyright/attribution notices and this scope map with redistributed covered material. For newly assembled bundles containing selected toolkit files, also retain the exact toolkit notice. New monograph/source bundles should include `MONOGRAPH-NOTICE.md`; this is also an accompanying notice for preserved Section 9 monograph downloads. Historical archive contents and scientific results are not rewritten to insert notices. Their present licensing scope and their historical computational or review status are separate questions.
