# Monograph source

The canonical text is [Polyhedra, Moduli, and Quantum Geometry](polyhedral_volumes_and_geodesic_identities.tex). It was imported byte-for-byte from the supplied 30 October 2026 archive. [Section 9](chapters/09-volume-hessians.tex) was revised following two independent mathematical audits, with its original labels and numbered formulas preserved, expanded coordinate and positivity proofs, explicit finite-centering guarantees, and precise geometric hypotheses.

The [current normalization checkpoint](../docs/execution/normalization-review-2026-11-01/README.md) separates Sections 1–8 and Appendix A into chapter files and reviews their measure conventions, geometric hypotheses, cone walls and flat limits. Its ledgers cover 41 claims: [Sections 1–6 and Appendix A](claims/sections1-6.json) and [Sections 7–8](claims/sections7-8.json). The [exact companion](../experiments/normalizations/README.md) checks fixed normalization, compactification, partition, Gram/Pfaffian and trace identities. Independent computational review passed; the checkpoint records mathematical review and integrated PDF verification separately. Finite arithmetic agreement does not prove external geometric results or analytic convergence.

Build with TeX Live and latexmk from this directory:

```sh
latexmk -pdf -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error -outdir=build main.tex
```

The wrapper `main.tex` delegates to the canonical source, which inputs the separate Sections 1–9, 15, 30 and Appendix A. The latest extraction preserved the expanded source byte-for-byte and all 607 labels before mathematical edits. The [reference PDF](reference/polyhedral_volumes_and_geodesic_identities.pdf) is the supplied PDF, not a newly reviewed release. New full-book build outputs go to the ignored `build/` directory. At the repository root, this checkpoint's output paths are `artifacts/normalization-review-2026-11-01/monograph-working-draft.pdf` and `artifacts/normalizations/result.json`; the checkpoint records their generation and verification.

The TikZ figures remain inline in the canonical TeX files, with editable copies in `figures/`. Editing only a separate figure copy does not change the PDF. Their captions and labels are retained.
