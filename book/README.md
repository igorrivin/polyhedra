# Monograph source

The canonical text is [Polyhedra, Moduli, and Quantum Geometry](polyhedral_volumes_and_geodesic_identities.tex). It was imported byte-for-byte from the supplied 30 October 2026 archive. [Section 9](chapters/09-volume-hessians.tex) was revised following two independent mathematical audits, with its original labels and numbered formulas preserved, expanded coordinate and positivity proofs, explicit finite-centering guarantees, and precise geometric hypotheses.

The subsequent Section 10.1 revision adds a physics box (Pauli matrices, angular momentum, Wigner recoupling and a measurement example), Euclidean Regge calculus, the Ponzano–Regge asymptotic, and the motivation and gluing rules for Turaev–Viro. A regular-tetrahedron companion compares exact 6j-values with their leading approximation. See the [Regge/Turaev–Viro revision record](../docs/execution/regge-turaev-viro-2026-11-03/README.md). All 613 existing label numbers and the original 129 bibliography numbers are retained; seven references are appended. Historical computational and acceptance records keep their original scope.

The 3 November exposition revision expands Sections 10.1 and 17.1 in response to reader feedback. It introduces the purpose and notation of representation-theoretic recoupling, computes a complete three-spin-one-half change of basis, and develops unfolding from periodic sums on the line to the punctured-torus integral and Mirzakhani's recursion. Existing labels and numbered equations remain unchanged. The [revision record](../docs/execution/exposition-revision-2026-11-03/README.md) records the examples, source checks, and separate build/publication evidence. Earlier independent acceptance records remain attached to their original source snapshots; they do not certify this new exposition.
