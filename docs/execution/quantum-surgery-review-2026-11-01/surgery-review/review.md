## Mathematical checks

- **Coordinates and volume factors.** The multiplier logs, eigenvalue-log conversion, smooth equation, core orientation and normalization chain `Phi_NZ=2F`, `Phi=Phi0+F/2` agree. Direct algebra gives `2(V-V0)=Im(S*)`; differentiating gives the displayed envelope and exact curvature formulas. The real Legendre subtraction yields `j=ell_core/2` precisely where a geometric cone completion exists. [Neumann–Zagier, §§4–6](https://people.mpim-bonn.mpg.de/zagier/files/doi/10.1016/0040-9383(85)90004-7/fulltext.pdf), [Wong–Yang, p.456](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/51093).
- **Angle space.** On the full affine angle tangent, the leading–trailing intersection pairing gives rank two per cusp, while the positive tetrahedral volume Hessian gives `K>0`. The constrained implicit-function argument and internal-edge stationarity therefore apply. The source filling theorem is used only at `theta=2pi`. The manuscript explicitly retains a holonomy interpretation otherwise. [Futer–Guéritaud, Lemma 4.4, Proposition 4.6, Lemma 5.3, Proposition 5.4, Theorem 6.1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1004.0440v2).
- **Uniform inverse.** The stated argument is sufficient: choose a fixed radius `r` with `sup|h'| <= B/2`. For every `|t|<=1/B` and `|z|<=r/2`, `u -> z-t h(u)` maps the closed radius-`r` disk into itself and has contraction constant at most one half. Analytic dependence, oddness and the real analytic volume formula then give the bounded-theta long-slope remainder. This argument neither constructs a cone completion nor provides quantum-level uniformity.
- **Formal algebra and figure-eight benchmark.** Independent exact Laurent-polynomial substitution confirms the inverse through degree five, action through degree six, SL2 determinant-one covariance, canonical generating identity and half-density squared ratio. Eliminating the shape variable recovers the stated cosh relation. Its coefficients independently give `v=2 i sqrt(3)(u+u^3/3+23u^5/180+...)`, including the required sign in `l=-exp(v/2)`. No factor repair is needed.
- **Hessians.** Independent rational calculations confirm the displayed figure-eight `E`, `K`, peripheral response, and corrected quartic tensor for six slopes including both signs of `q` and `q=0`. Generic polynomial identities check the one-cusp determinant, complex structure and compatible metric. For several cusps the block factorization by `[[I,-A],[0,I]]`, `diag(B,B^-1)` and its transpose proves positivity and determinant `4^-h`; noncommuting two- and three-cusp rational fixtures confirm the matrix ordering. These calculations concern the stipulated local angle space.
- **Torsion and phase.** The explicit acyclicity, peripheral twisted-homology bases, nonzero derivative and loxodromic core are appropriate. Porti's chamber regularity requires some peripheral element with distinct eigenvalues, not the filling curve itself; thus a trivial filling-slope holonomy is not an objection when the core is loxodromic. The moving adjoint core eigenvalues give `(1-e^w)(1-e^-w)=-4 sinh^2(w/2)`, explaining the stated surgery factor up to torsion sign. Square-root phases and the extra solid-torus factor remain necessary. [Porti, Propositions 5.13–5.14 and 4.23(b)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1511.00400v2), [Wong–Yang, (5.16), (5.23)](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/51093).
- **Quantum scope.** The conditional transfer includes the needed exact representation, symbol, nondegeneracy, cycle, net nonvanishing and global exponential-gap assumptions. The cusp endpoint and simultaneous slope/level limit remain excluded without extra analysis. The general statement stays conjectural; the cited figure-eight leading RT theorem and RT/TV limits are genuine external special-case results. The state-integral kernel reference does not establish a general RT identification. [Chen–Yang, Conjectures 1.1–1.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1503.02547v4), [Wong–Yang, Theorems 1.1–1.2](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/51093), [Gang–Romo–Yamazaki, (12)–(14), footnote 16](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1704.00918v2).
