    About this working edition

    A chapter with its computational trail

    Section 9 has a bounded mathematical review and a separate review of its computational addendum. The claim ledger distinguishes proofs, external theorem inputs, finite checks, and open questions. The remaining monograph is a broader draft; this edition does not extend Section 9’s review to the whole book.

    The companions expose their inputs, methods, precision, results, and runnable archives. They do not provide a rigorous floating-point certificate or a global geometric realization algorithm.
