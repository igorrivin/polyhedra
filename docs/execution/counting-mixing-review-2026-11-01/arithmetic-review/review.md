## Analytic findings

The substantive arguments are sound under their explicit hypotheses. Their conclusions are not strengthened by the finite numerical campaign.

| Claim group | Independent assessment |
| --- | --- |
| Normalization, Möbius inversion, Dini bound | Signed primitive vectors and boundary inclusion are consistent. The lattice minimum gives the exact floor cutoff. The normalized area tail is uniformly bounded. The decreasing envelope supplies the harmonic-integral comparison, with the interval below one treated separately. The Dini condition gives an absolute-value `O(L)` result; it does not require cancellation. |
| Strict-convexity improvement | Endpoint chords vanish because the supporting faces are singletons. Concavity plus endpoint continuity gives the zero-extended chord function an integrable derivative. Fixed-direction Fourier decay suffices: the Poisson sum is dominated after fixing the physical smoothing radius. Taking the dilation limit before the radius limit proves all-point `o(L)` and then primitive `o(L log L)`. No uniform directional decay rate, curvature bound, or flatness preprint is silently used. |
| Finite and infinite dyadic inversion | The finite remainder is exactly `4^(-k) E(2^k L)`. The quadratic asymptotic is required to remove it. The `O(L)`, `O(L log L)`, and all-point `O(L/log L)` equivalences follow from absolutely convergent geometric tails under their stated infinite-range hypotheses. A finite table does not establish those hypotheses. |
| Event endpoints | Both full and half events are required. Simultaneous jumps must be grouped. The actual value at the initial endpoint is included; its external left limit is excluded. Both terminal one-sided values matter for the supremum; endpoints have zero measure for the integral. The exact fixture `[2,2,4,5,8]`, `T=2`, gives `M=5/2`, `Q²=49/20`. |
| Flat row and height | The two-sided graph, positive height, irrational radial contact, rational lattice normal, and strict convexity are explicit. Compactness localizes shallow caps. Inverse widths are concave. Prime rows lose at most one multiple of the row index after their width is smaller than that index. | Flatness models and all-point compatibility | The three model inversions have the stated orders and are convex sufficiently near the origin. They are illustrative profiles, not identified torus geometries. Infinite flatness excludes a fixed sublinear power under the contact hypotheses, but does not give literal `Omega(L)`. The all-point row estimate needs no prime restriction or totient factor; it correctly makes `W(h/L²)=O(1/log L)` necessary for the proposed logarithmic target. | Zero-free half-plane equivalence | The zero-free logarithm disk excludes the pole at large height. The half-integer Perron contour at `T=x²` yields the claimed Mertens exponent with arbitrarily small loss. The reciprocal has no pole at `s=1`. Local uniform convergence of the reverse Mellin integral proves the converse. The argument uses special analytic properties of zeta; it is not asserted for arbitrary Dirichlet series. |
| Conditional optimization | The logarithmic comparison, all three VK rows, and power balance are correct. The cutoff exponent lies strictly between zero and one. At `alpha=2/3`, the limiting arithmetic exponents give `10/11`, `34/35`, and `50/51`. No geometric power estimate is assigned to a McShane–Rivin ball. |

## Computational review and repairs

The three open Farey sectors, with roots inserted separately, provide disjoint primitive slope coverage modulo simultaneous sign. Determinants and checked coordinate additions preserve primitiveness and avoid signed-coordinate overflow. Equal traces retain distinct curves. The exact fixture roots satisfy the Markoff equation and the reduced inequalities. The initial outward subtraction ratios are at most one half; subsequent ratios are smaller than the reciprocal of a root trace. This proves the mathematical monotonicity used for pruning. Numerical pruning remains subject to rounding uncertainty.

The logarithmic update and length conversion avoid exponential overflow. Underflow of negligible correction terms is allowed and belongs to the numerical error budget. Measured `double` and `long double` precision is 53 bits on this host; neither the code nor report treats `long double` as a second precision. The independent references use scoped Decimal precision.

The full/half event merge, exact equality grouping, endpoint handling, Kahan integration, and exact rational maximum comparisons are correct. The four uncertainty streams implement the stated lower/upper counting envelopes. For binary64 lengths in the stated range, `2^53 * 5^6` represents both full and half epsilon endpoints exactly. Numerator, count, discrepancy, and checked-product bounds suffice for signed 128-bit comparisons. Exactness here concerns the finite rational envelopes; it does not certify transcendental lengths, the uniform epsilon hypothesis, or completeness under perturbation.

The all-point routine uses exact comparisons against stored binary64 rationals. The source records the independent identity stream separately from the sorted length stream and expressly states that their indices are not aligned. SHA256 identities supplement the deterministic FNV stream hash. The source/input hash list includes all four campaign files and the actual old oracle, fixture, and frozen result. The driver records the dirty state and HEAD, rechecks source/input hashes and HEAD before final output, requires matching-source 512 preflight for a full run, and uses exclusive output creation. A clean-source full run is a separate gate. These provenance controls were inspected; no source-drift mutation was injected into the canonical checkout.

See [malformed-probes.json](malformed-probes.json).

## Limits and integration decision

The strongest remaining challenge to a numerical interpretation is unsampled error or omitted membership near a threshold, together with possible large excursions beyond the finite range. The report already states these limitations. The dense finite event sweep and independent matrix samples support the reported finite diagnostics; they do not remove the challenge or imply an asymptotic bound.

The full v2 counts `18,191,508` and `18,486,116` and the four historically printed M/Q rows agree with the historical report. This does not recover historical code, the original sample selection, or the historical precision/sensitivity construction. In particular, the new worst observed native residual is roughly `1.2e-9`, not the historical claimed `7.5e-13`; the new maximum envelope width across **all** rows is about `1.2313e-6` for the modular torus. The smaller width on the historically printed subset is a different quantity. Integration must keep those descriptions separate.

No independent full traversal replay was performed: the independently written full-stream count arithmetic and long matrix-word checks resolved distinct verification needs without duplicating the author’s traversal. The approved final source still needs the planned full clean-source replay after its checkpoint. The author’s final 512 preflight is suitable for that same-source gate. New numerical prose and curated full artifacts should cite that final replay once available. The currently frozen Section 30 prose still calls the historical report unreplayed by design; it was not treated as a defect in this pre-integration review.

There is no asymptotic theorem, every-node interval certificate, identified flat irrational rational-normal point on either named torus, recent preprint proof validation, or publication/deployment claim in this approval.
