The full-run raw files remain under the outer execution directory, in `torus-campaign/full8192-author-v1`, `torus-campaign/full8192-author-v2`, and `clean-source/torus8192`. Each run has four streams totaling 586,841,984 bytes. Modular streams each contain 18,191,508 records and 145,532,064 bytes; asymmetric streams each contain 18,486,116 records and 147,888,928 bytes. The `.lengths.f64le` stream is sorted IEEE-754 binary64 little-endian length data, 8 bytes per record. The `.slopes.i32le` stream has little-endian signed int32 pairs `(p,q)`, also 8 bytes per record, in root/sector/depth-first enumeration order. The two streams are **not index-aligned**. Full replay regenerates them; indexed pairing of the two files would be incorrect.

The [raw identity manifest](raw-stream-retention.json) records exact paths, hashes, byte counts and the distinction between historical report identities and freshly verified v2/final-run identities. The [strict torus comparison](clean-source/torus/scientific-comparison.json) freshly hashed both v2 and final native reports and all four streams per run, and rechecked the identities before success. The final checkpoint verifier repeats those identity checks. Native executables stay outer with recorded compiler arguments and executable hashes; the result and compact native reports are retained here.

The [copy manifest](evidence-copy-manifest.json) is the exact boundary of curation. Source and PDF approval receipts precede this candidate and can be retained without a cycle. The subsequent independent candidate decision and postcommit verification must stay outer, because they bind the candidate manifest or artifact commit.
