# Independent source audit for Sections 23–25

Reviewed 1 November 2026 by `/root/counting_algebra_reviewer`. These are primary-source statement, convention, and dependency checks. They do not re-referee the complete external proofs. Exact mathematical derivations in the review remain separate from the external inputs.

| Ledger source | Independent access and checked scope |
| --- | --- |
| CharlesMarche | [arXiv:0901.3064v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0901.3064v1), introduction and Theorem 1.1, printed p. 4. The multicurve functions form the trace-algebra basis for SU(2) and SL(2,C); the source uses minus trace per component. Its introduction states the coordinate-ring consequence. The chapter's unnegated basis differs only by signs. |
| NaudTwisted | [arXiv:2209.07941v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2209.07941v1), Fourier convention p. 2 and Theorem 2.2 p. 6. The sum is over primitive conjugacy classes, with denominator `2 sinh`; the identity multiplier is `(g-1) dim(V)`. The neighboring proposition distinguishes a class from its inverse, confirming that inverse classes have not already been grouped. |
| DThurstonPositive | [arXiv:1310.1959v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1310.1959v2), Definition 1.1 and Theorem 2 p. 1; Proposition 4.4 and basis definitions/proposition p. 5. Checked the spin identification, canonical twisted Fuchsian representation, sign coherence for every diagram, and Chebyshev bracelets. Positivity of the bracelet evaluation follows from the positive hyperbolic length trace. No claim of rechecking the entire positivity proof. |
| BasokChelkak | [publisher PDF](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/32573), Theorems 4.7 and 4.9, printed p. 2821, and the following Chebyshev argument. These are related sign-coherence and coefficient bounds in the punctured planar setup. The chapters correctly do not import the full analytic expansion theorem into the closed-surface setting. |
| EMMEffective | [arXiv:1905.04435v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1905.04435v2), Theorem 1.1 p. 2; Lemma 5.1 and integral points p. 27; Theorem 5.1 p. 29; final partition paragraph p. 45. Verified the fixed rational multicurve power-saving statement, lattice correspondence, convexity of length in a train-track cone, and finite-sided overlap partition. The nonzero condition in the chapter is appropriate. No numerical exponent or complete mixing proof was checked. |
| MirzSimple | Direct opening of the [journal PDF](https://www.labri.fr/perso/vdelecro/toprint/mirzakhani4-pdf2up.pdf) timed out again. I did not independently reproduce the author's journal-search text. The [11 April 2004 primary preprint](https://www.math.stonybrook.edu/~mlyubich/Archive/Geometry/Teichmuller%20Space/Mirz1.pdf), printed p. 8, Theorem 3.1, was directly recovered: all integral multicurves have leading coefficient given by the lattice-normalized length ball. Its printed p. 9 also records the Dehn-coordinate parity condition. This confirms the substantive qualitative input but not the journal proposition number by direct page access. |
| LuoStongLengths | [arXiv:math/0211421v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0211421v1), Theorem 1.1 printed p. 497 and Corollary 1.2 p. 498. Checked coordinate length estimates and extension to measured laminations. Proposition 2.2 also visibly retains pants parity constraints. The chapter does not assert that a surface-specific numerical inradius comes precomputed from this source. |
| McShaneRivinTori | [arXiv:math/0005220v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0005220v1), norm discussion and Theorem 4.1 p. 8. The displayed count is the primitive-vector count in the full norm ball. Grouping opposite vectors accounts for the chapter's additional factor one half. This source is used as a separate punctured-torus benchmark. |
| RivinFigures | [arXiv:0901.2543v4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0901.2543v4), Theorem 1.1 p. 4, Theorem 1.3 p. 5, and punctured-torus discussion p. 7. Checked the one-crossing pants neighborhood, positive hyperbolic pants trace identity, and repeated cuff images. No counting theorem or asymptotic proof from this paper is needed for the reviewed filter or gap. |
| AHTComponents | [arXiv:math/0205057v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0205057v2), Theorem 12 p. 14 and Corollary 13 p. 18. The stated algorithm counts components of a normal curve in time polynomial in triangulation size times logarithmic normal weight. It does not directly count distinct supports with parallel multiplicities suppressed. The chapter's valid integral-division step addresses that distinction. |
| CdVPeriodic | [Numdam primary scan](https://www.numdam.org/item/CM_1973__27_2_159_0.pdf), introduction and Section 1, printed pp. 159–162. The broken-geodesic approximation and critical-point/energy correspondence are present. The normal Hessian and equal-time normalization were independently derived in the review rather than taken from imperfect formula OCR. |
| WolpertTwist | Initial direct PDF extraction returned only the cover and the screenshot tool returned no inspectable image. A primary search result then opened the [same GDZ scan with `#page=1`](https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/download/pdf/PPN358147735_0056/LOG_0014.pdf#page=1), exposing full text. Thus this review independently recovers the formula that the author freeze honestly marked unretrieved. The alternate e-periodica endpoint required verification and was not used. |
| AougabLahnLovingMiller | [arXiv:2210.16706v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2210.16706v2), introduction and Corollary 2 p. 2. Verified existence of unmarked length-isospectral hyperbolic surfaces with distinct bounded-self-intersection length spectra, including simple length spectra. This does not obstruct a marked, labelled family of unitary twists. |
| RivinSimpleAsymptotics | [arXiv:math/0512066v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0512066v1), introduction and initial multicurve discussion. Read as background only. I do not endorse the complete proof of this background note. |
