The root agent owns `experiments/counting_mixing/` and the new result artifact. All finite ranges below are suggested and must be recorded in the eventual result.

1. **A scalar path count misses a periodic Markov chain.** Section 26, `subsec:countmixscalar`; baseline main lines 4323–4340. For integers `m=2,3,5`, let `A=[[0,m],[m,0]]`, `P=A/m`, and `pi=(1/2,1/2)`. For `n=1,...,32`, verify in exact arithmetic `1^T A^n 1=2 m^n`, `tr(A^n)=m^n+(-m)^n`, and `E_pi[f(X_0)f(X_n)]=(-1)^n` for `f=(1,-1)`. This is a finite replay of an explicit counterexample, not a numerical test of a sufficient counting theorem.

2. **Trace gap and terminal differentiation.** Section 26 `prop:countmixfinite` and Section 27 `eq:twopointfullidentity`. For `A=[[2,1],[1,2]]`, verify `tr(A^n)=3^n+1`, `P^n-Pi=3^{-n}(I-Pi)`. An exact symbolic family for the latter identity is `C(t)=M+c exp(-r t)` with positive rational `h,r` and `r!=h`: integrating `exp(h t)(h+partial_t)(C(t)-M)` from 1 to T gives `exp(hT)(C(T)-M)-exp(h)(C(1)-M)`. Include the removable `r=h` case separately. This checks finite algebra, not that an arbitrary count has the required remainder.

3. **Two endpoints and changing frequency.** Section 27 `eq:pairbruhat`, `eq:pairkloosterman`, `eq:pairkloostermansmooth`; baseline lines 5226–5290. For positive `c=2,...,31`, enumerate units `a mod c` and inverses `d`. Verify `ad=1 mod c`, the Bruhat matrix product with `b=(ad-1)/c`, and exact residue multiplicities. For integer Fourier frequencies `m,n`, the normalized endpoint statistic is `S(m,n;c)/phi(c)`, where `S=sum_a exp(2 pi i (m a+n d)/c)`. Use high precision or exact cyclotomic arithmetic with that status declared. The adaptive test `m=n=c` has zero continuous endpoint means but statistic exactly 1. Do not call this a counterexample to the smooth norm bound: its norms grow with c.

4. **Primitive parity geometry.** Section 29 `lem:polearcprimitivebound`, `eq:polearcflatlength`. Enumerate primitive integer vectors for several fixed rational unimodular matrices and explicit bounded R; retain all three nonzero parity classes. Check the parity permutation under `Gamma(2)` basis changes, the sign-pair multiplicity, determinant integrality, and the finite bound `count<=2+2 pi R^2`. A search for vectors must use a proven finite coordinate bound, e.g. `|m|<=||g^{-1}|| R`, not an arbitrary box. These checks do not establish the generic genus-two arc lift or a hyperbolic-length comparison.

5. **Affine Gaussian isometry and rotation norms.** Section 29 `eq:affinesiegelexactcorr`, `eq:affinegaussiancorr`. For `f_ab(x,y)=exp(-pi(a x^2+b y^2))`, `a,b>0`, derive/check `||f_ab||_2^2=1/(2 sqrt(ab))`, `D_J f_ab=2 pi(a-b)xy f_ab`, and `||D_J f_ab||_2^2=(a-b)^2/[8(ab)^(3/2)]`. Hence `S_1(F_f)^2=(a+b)^2/[8(ab)^(3/2)]` through the exact affine isometry. The same-test flow covariance is `1/[2 sqrt(ab) cosh(t)]`; `a=b=1` is the manuscript formula. Two different relative-mark centered transforms have covariance zero by fiber orthogonality. Test rational pairs `(1,1),(1,2),(2,3)` and bounded times using independent Gaussian product integration. This is a moduli-averaged identity, not a sample of hyperbolic curve counts.

6. **Two-mark rank-one divergence.** Section 29 `subsec:affineranktwo`. The Gaussian collinear term is `[4 zeta(2)]^{-1} sum_{a,b!=0}(a^2+b^2)^{-1}`. Finite boxes can illustrate increasing truncated sums. They cannot prove divergence; the chapter's positive integral/shell argument supplies that proof. Never compare a truncated raw product as if it were an L2 observable.

7. **Exact full-rank Gaussian covariance.** Section 29 `eq:affineranktwogaussiancorr`. With `c=cosh t`, `q=exp(-4 pi c)`, covariance is `pi/[zeta(2)c] sum_{k>=1} sigma_1(k)q^k/k`. Independent forms are the determinant-orbit Gaussian integral `pi^2 exp(-2 alpha k)/[alpha k zeta(2)]`, `alpha=2 pi c`, and `-sum_{n>=1}log(1-q^n)`. A rigorous analytic majorant for the tail after N of the k-series is `q^(N+1)*((N+1)-N q)/(1-q)^2`, since `sigma_1(k)/k<=k`; multiply by the prefactor. For the log-series tail after N, use `sum_{n>N}-log(1-q^n)<=q^(N+1)/[(1-q)(1-q^(N+1))]`. Evaluated floating-point majorants are not interval-certified total errors. The zero-time value is an exact squared norm formula. Radial tests have rotation derivative zero, so this also checks the stated rotation norm convention.

8. **Sharp rotation cost.** Section 29 `subsec:affinesharprotation`. For the fixed physical rectangle `[-a,a] x [-b,b]`, the coefficient of `|theta|` in its symmetric-difference area with its rotation is exactly `2(a^2+b^2)`. A disk centered at zero has identically zero rotation difference. Exact polygon clipping at selected angles, or independently computed area integrals, may illustrate the one-sided limit; finite angles do not prove the Sobolev threshold. This fixed-physical-region test is different from a hyperbolic-length cutoff invariant under phase rotation.

No fixture above proves the Otal–Rosas input, Cowling–Haagerup–Howe, arbitrary affine-quotient mixing, the nonsquare stratum classification, or a global coefficient-to-bisector comparison. Numerical quadratures must retain precision, truncation, residual, and backend distinctions.
