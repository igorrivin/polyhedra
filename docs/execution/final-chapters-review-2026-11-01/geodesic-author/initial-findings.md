# Initial read-only findings, Sections 17–22

Baseline `151673b1817e66aae493eb7da1be5ddf49adfe0f`, main source lines 1513–2274. No canonical edits were made for this preparatory record.

1. The algebraic formulas for pants traces, the all-figure kernel relation, torus twist integral, H/K partition, cusp truncation, and Basmajian orientation factor agree with direct derivations. They require the domains and conventions recorded in the companion contract.
2. Section 17 cites Mirzakhani Theorem 8.1 for the integration mechanism. The published primary source instead gives the general integration formula in Theorem 7.1, with Lemma 7.3 and equations (7.3)–(7.5) making the covering/symmetry mechanism explicit. The chapter citation needs correcting to Section 7/Theorem 7.1; Theorem 8.1 is the recursion.
3. Closure under differentiation must require locally uniform convergence of the derivative series, plus convergence at one parameter value (or a suitable dominated C1 family), rather than merely uniform convergence of the original series. The mean/variance test needs finite positive moduli volume, real-valued S (or absolute square for complex S), and full support to upgrade continuous a.e. constancy to everywhere. A vanishing differential gives constancy on each connected component.
4. The figure-eight classification should explicitly use an incompressible pants neighborhood, preserve the two distinct N-type free homotopy classes, and explain uniqueness of the disjoint torus simple curve. The positive trace convention must not be confused with the signed trace variables of reviewed Section 23. The local cover's lifts are simple individually and intersect each other twice.
5. An explicit distance Hessian gives a direct proof of strict convexity for one twist and works for immersed curves. Fix the reduced itinerary and boundary lifts, all local distances d>0, and all other twists. The local Hessian is positive definite. Endpoint positions satisfy affine matching constraints. Distinct values of a genuinely crossed twist produce distinct full endpoint vectors, so strict convexity survives minimization by interpolating the two minimizing endpoint configurations. A disjoint cuff is the constant-length exception. No stronger multi-twist theorem is needed.
6. The gap-ray discussion in Section 22 must be scoped to complete finite-area surfaces without geodesic boundary. On a surface with geodesic boundary, an open family of simple cusp-issued rays can exit through that boundary, so “almost every direction eventually develops intersections” is false in that larger scope. The original McShane author draft Theorem 3 states the no-boundary finite-area hypotheses explicitly.
7. Add finite analytic tails for F iteration, H lattice partition, Fermi integral, and cosh-power twist integrals. Do not propose numerical approximation of unrestricted surface spectra without a separate enumeration and global tail estimate.
