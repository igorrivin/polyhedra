B Conventions at a glance

These conventions locate the normalizations used in the chapter formulas. Their geometric and analytic hypotheses remain those of the referenced statements; a convention does not assert an existence or convergence theorem.

Use this table to check a convention after finding its definition in the linked section. For example, a cone weight μ=1/3\mu=1/3 means deficit 2π/32\pi/3 and opening 4π/34\pi/3, while an ideal tetrahedron’s interior dihedral angle π/3\pi/3 means exterior angle 2π/32\pi/3. Equal numbers in these two examples describe different geometric objects. Similarly jj below labels a quantum representation, while the later filling-source jj is a different variable with its own definition.
