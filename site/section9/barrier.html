Following the log-sine barrier path

The computation and its guarantee

The affine coordinates are α = α₀ + Cu. The solver minimizes Φt = F − tV, with F = −∑ log sin α. It increases t by at most 1 + 1/(20√m), then takes a full Newton step, where m is the corner-angle count.

V* − V(u) ≤ [m + (√m + λ)λ/(1 − λ)]/t ≤ 17m/(12t), when λ ≤ 1/4.

The displayed inequality is the chapter’s exact-arithmetic theorem. Here derivatives, linear solves, decrements and the bound are evaluated in binary64. The reported numbers are numerical estimates, not rigorous floating-point certificates. The independent 80-digit comparisons below test these fixed inputs and do not enclose all rounding errors.

Initial centering work is counted separately. Positivity, conditioning, linear residuals and endpoint centering are checked explicitly; a numerical or budget failure is reported rather than clipped into apparent success. This dense method makes no nearly-linear complexity claim and does not certify an embedding.
